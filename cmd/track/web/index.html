    orbitscreen

    Every object in the public satellite catalog, moving in real time. Positions come from SGP4, written from scratch in Go, and on this site the same Go code runs in your browser, compiled to WebAssembly.

    The project's main job is screening: finding every pair of objects that will pass within 5 km of each other.

    • Whole catalog, 7 days, 5 minutes on a laptop: all 32,591 objects in orbit, ~136× faster than checking every pair.
    • 100% of CelesTrak SOCRATES's close approaches found on the same data (205,716 of 205,716), with miss distances within 0.5 m.

    Try Close approaches for the next 24 hours, click any dot, or search by name. These are screening results from public orbit data, accurate to about a kilometre: worth a closer look, not collision predictions.
