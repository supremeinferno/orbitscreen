    orbitscreen

    Every object in the public satellite catalog, moving in real time. Positions come from SGP4, written from scratch in Go. On the public site, that Go code runs in your browser, compiled to WebAssembly.

    The project's main job is screening: finding every pair of objects that will pass within 5 km of each other.

    • Whole catalog, 7 days, 5 minutes on a laptop: all 32,591 objects in orbit, ~136× faster than checking every pair.
    • 100% of CelesTrak SOCRATES's close approaches found on the same data (205,716 of 205,716), with miss distances within 0.5 m.

    Its sister project, satnogs-pipeline, listens to five small satellites and checks their batteries against sunlight and the Earth's shadow. Search for UWE-4, CatSat, SNUGLITE, Sharjahsat-1 or GRBBeta to see theirs here.

    Open Close approaches for the next 24 hours, click any dot, or search by name. Drag to turn the globe; scroll to zoom out to the geostationary ring. Keys: / search, Space pause, Esc clear.

    These are screening results from public orbit data, accurate to about a kilometre: worth a closer look, not collision predictions.
